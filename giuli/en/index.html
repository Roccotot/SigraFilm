Why GiuLi

Made by people who know projection booths

GiuLi comes from Sigra Film's daily work in cinemas: it does what is really needed, without becoming one more thing to manage.

🛡️

The evening does not depend on the TMS

Shows are written into the schedule of the screen servers: the servers play them. If the GiuLi PC goes down, the evening goes on.

👁️

Watches without touching

Device status, messages and data are read-only. Commands are sent only when you ask, with a confirmation where it matters.

⚡

Ready in five minutes

One folder and a double click on Windows: no installation, no database to set up. It opens from any browser on the network and starts again by itself when the PC is switched on.

🎬

One cinema or a whole circuit

Each cinema keeps its own data and credentials. A single page shows every screen of every site, grouped by city.

Features

Everything happening in the booth, on one page

From the week's schedule to the exact time of each screen server: GiuLi brings together information that today is spread across different software.

📺Live dashboard

What each screen is playing, how far it is, when it ends and what starts next. Markers for the start of the feature, the intermission and the end credits, with the time and a countdown. Devices with their icon and the address that opens their web page.

🖥️Monitor

The playlists of every playing screen, side by side on a single page. Each one follows the clip being played.

🎛️Screen controls

Play, pause, stop, jumps and playlist loading. The server's quick commands become buttons with an icon: volume, Flat and Scope, HDMI, douser, lamp, lights. Sound, lamp, douser and power of Barco Series 4 from Eco.

🗓️Scheduling

A timeline per screen: drag a show to another time, repeat it by picking the days on the calendar, swap two screens. Everything goes to the server after checking playlist, KDMs, clips and overlaps. Refresh reads everything again from the servers.

🎟️From box office to server

The week's sessions come from the box office file. Link each film to its playlist once, and "Schedule all" sends them to the servers.

⏱️Automations

A playlist before the first show and one after the last, in every screen, every day. If the schedule changes, GiuLi puts them back in place by itself.

🎞️Playlists

A list with the film, the scheduled shows and the KDM validity. In the editor you drag contents filtered by type, blacks of any length and the server's quick commands to the exact point. Playlists already on the screen servers open and can be edited too.

📦Blocks and screen blocks

Groups of clips, such as the week's trailers, dropped into the playlist in one go. A screen block changes with the screen the playlist goes to.

⏸️Doremi Intermission

The intermission in the middle of the film: at a point of the feature the server plays another playlist and then resumes, even going back a few seconds. For Doremi and IMS3000.

💿Content and copies

The DCPs of every server, of the TMS and of the NAS, with KDM validity and those not used in any playlist. Copy to several screens, to the TMS or to a USB drive in one go: copies are queued and run together whenever they can. When a playlist goes to a screen, the missing contents start by themselves.

🗄️NAS and FTP folders

The distributors' FTP folders and the cinema's NAS, read with all their subfolders, even a thousand DCPs. From there you copy to the screens or to the TMS and see at once which screens already have them.

💽TMS on several disks

TMS storage on several disks, without RAID, seen as a single folder. Each disk copies on its own, so two films arrive together; versions of the same film (OV and VF, Italian and English) stay on the same disk.

🔌USB drives and disks

Plug a USB stick or a disk into the PC, internal ones too: GiuLi shows DCPs, KDMs and playlists, first those not yet on the TMS, and copies them with one click. And the other way round: a playlist goes to the USB drive together with its contents.

🔑KDMs

Validity per screen and upload to the servers. KDMs received by email go by themselves to the right server; those for other cinemas are recognised.

⚠️Device messages

Errors, warnings and notifications of projectors and servers, with the same text their own software shows: maintenance, NTP, disks, lamp. Even from projectors without a web page.

🙈Alerts without the noise

The alerts of all sites in one list with filters for city, cinema, type, level, code and device. A known error, such as a projector being installed, is hidden with one click and no longer shows up anywhere, not even on Telegram.

🎚️Sound configuration

The configuration of Dolby CP750 processors, equalisation included, and of CP850 and CP950, saved on the PC by itself, read-only: ready if a processor has to be replaced or reset to factory settings.

📈Diagnostics

Disk space of the TMS and of the servers, lamp or laser hours with the lifetime bar, software versions, boards and serial numbers, alerts screen by screen.

🗺️Global, city by city

Every screen of every cinema on one page, grouped by city: what is playing, what starts next, what does not answer. The cinema menu is by city too.

📨Telegram alerts

Problems in real time on your phone and, every evening, the summary of the next day for each cinema.

🕒Server clocks

GiuLi compares the time of every server with the PC and warns you if shows would start early or late.

👥Users and log

Read-only, operator and administrator roles. An event log with filters and CSV export.

💾Automatic backup

Every night a copy of all data, sound processor configurations included; the last 14 days are kept. To move a cinema, just copy its folder.

🔁Starts by itself

When the PC is switched on, GiuLi starts by itself, even without logging in to Windows: after a power cut, the screens are back under control without anyone doing anything.

Projector and server messages

Know what is wrong before the audience does

GiuLi reads the active messages of Barco and Cinemeccanica projectors and of Doremi and Dolby servers, with the same code and text as the manufacturer's software, even from projectors without a web page. Errors are red, warnings yellow, notifications only informative.

  • A badge on every screen: hover it to read all the messages
  • Scheduled maintenance stays yellow: it does not stop the show
  • New errors and warnings reach Telegram too
  • The alerts of all sites in one list with filters; known ones are hidden with one click

Quick commands

The server's commands, at a glance

GiuLi recognises the quick commands of the screen servers by their name and shows them as buttons with an icon: volumes as small squares with the number, Flat and Scope, HDMI, douser open or closed, lamp, lights, play and pause. The words it recognises them by can be changed in Automations.

  • Families with many commands, such as 26 volumes, become a drop-down: pick one and it runs
  • The same buttons on the Dashboard and on the Controls page
  • Every device with its icon: projector, server, sound processor

  • NAS and FTP folders read with all their subfolders, even a thousand DCPs
  • TMS on several disks without RAID: each disk copies on its own
  • Versions of the same film stay on the same disk

  • The source is never touched: it is copied, not moved

Getting started

Three steps and you are running

Copy and start

GiuLi runs on a Windows PC on the cinema network. A double click and it starts, also by itself when the PC is switched on.

Add screens and devices

For each screen the address of server, projector and sound processor. GiuLi connects and starts reading them right away.

Check from anywhere

From the booth PC, the office or the phone, on the cinema network or over VPN. Everyone with their own user.

FAQ

The questions we hear most

What happens if the GiuLi PC goes down?

Shows already scheduled start anyway: they are in the schedule of the screen servers. When the PC is back on, GiuLi starts reading everything again by itself.

Can GiuLi stop a show or switch off a projector by mistake?

No. GiuLi does not run commands by itself: manual commands ask for confirmation and respect the server's schedule lock. A projector with the light on is never sent to Eco.

And if there is a power cut?

The shows are already on the screen servers. When the PC is switched back on, GiuLi starts by itself, even if nobody logs in to Windows, and goes back to reading everything.

Does it need an internet connection?

No, GiuLi works on the cinema network. Internet is only needed for Telegram alerts and KDMs received by email.

What computer does it need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC switched on in the cinema network. Nothing to install: the first start sets everything up by itself.

Can I manage several cinemas?

Yes. Each cinema has its own folder with data and credentials, and the Global page shows every screen of every site, grouped by city.

I already use another TMS: what changes?

Playlists and schedules stay on the screen servers. GiuLi reads them, including those with Doremi Intermission, shows them in scheduling with the KDM and clip checks, opens them for editing and can copy them between screens or import them into its own playlists.

What language is it in?

Italian and English: switch with the flag at the top, on the login page too.

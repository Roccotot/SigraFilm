Why GiuLi

Built by people who know projection booths

GiuLi comes out of Sigra Film's everyday work in cinemas: it does what is really needed, without becoming one more thing to look after.

🛡️

The show does not depend on the TMS

Shows are written into the schedule of each screen server, and the servers play them. If the GiuLi PC goes off, the evening goes on.

👁️

It looks, it does not touch

Status, messages and device data are read-only. Commands are sent only when you ask, with a confirmation where it matters.

⚡

Up and running in five minutes

One folder and a double click on Windows: nothing to install, no database to set up. Open it from any browser on the network.

🎬

One cinema or a circuit

Each cinema keeps its own data and credentials. A single page shows every screen of every site.

Features

Everything happening in the booth, on one page

From this week's schedule to the exact time of each screen server: GiuLi brings together information that today is scattered across different programs.

📺Live dashboard

What each screen is playing, how far along it is, when it ends and what comes next. Markers for feature start, intermission and end credits, with time and countdown.

🖥️Monitor

The playlists of every screen that is playing, side by side on one display. Each one follows the clip on screen by itself.

🎛️Screen controls

Play, pause, stop, skips and playlist loading. The server's quick commands (macros), sound volume and input, lamp, dowser and waking Barco Series 4 projectors from Eco.

🗓️Scheduling

Timeline per screen: drag a show to another time, repeat it by picking days on the calendar, swap two screens. Everything goes to the server after checking playlist, KDMs and overlaps.

🎟️From box office to server

The week's sessions come from the box office file. Match each film to its playlist once, and "Schedule all" sends them to the servers.

⏱️Automations

A playlist before the first show and one after the last, on every screen, every day. When the schedule changes, GiuLi puts them back in place by itself.

🎞️Playlist editor

List with feature, schedules and KDM validity. In the editor you drag content filtered by type, blacks of any length and the server's quick commands to the exact point.

📦Packages and screen packages

Groups of clips, like this week's trailers, dropped into a playlist in one go. A screen package changes with the screen the playlist goes to.

⏸️Doremi Intermission

A mid-feature intermission: at a point of the feature the server plays another playlist, then resumes, even going back a few seconds. For Doremi and IMS3000.

💿Content and copies

The DCPs on every server, with KDM validity and the ones not used in any playlist. Copy screen to screen or from the TMS; when a playlist goes to a screen, missing content is copied by itself.

🔌USB drives and disks

Plug a USB drive or a disk into the PC: GiuLi shows its DCPs, KDMs and playlists and copies them to the TMS in one click. And the other way round, from the TMS to the drive.

🔑KDMs

Validity per screen and upload to the servers. KDMs that arrive by email go to the right server by themselves; those for other cinemas are recognised.

⚠️Device messages

Errors, warnings and notifications from projectors and servers, with the same text their own software shows: maintenance, NTP, disks, lamp.

📈Reports

Disk space of the TMS and the servers, serial numbers, lamp hours and strikes, alerts screen by screen.

📨Telegram alerts

Real-time problems on your phone and, every evening, a summary of the next day for each cinema.

🕒Server clocks

GiuLi compares the time of every server with the PC and warns you if shows would start early or late.

👥Users and log

Read-only, operator and administrator roles. Event log with filters and CSV export.

💾Automatic backup

Every night a copy of all the data, with the last 14 days kept. To move a cinema, just copy its folder.

Projector and server messages

Know what is wrong before the audience does

GiuLi reads the active messages of Barco projectors and Doremi and Dolby servers, with the same code and the same text as the manufacturer's software. Errors are red, warnings yellow, notifications are for information only.

  • Scheduled maintenance stays yellow: it does not stop playback
  • New errors and warnings reach Telegram too

Getting started

Three steps and you are live

Copy and start

GiuLi runs on a Windows PC on the cinema network. A double click and it starts, also on its own when the PC boots.

Add screens and devices

For each screen, the address of the server, projector and sound processor. GiuLi connects and starts reading them right away.

Keep an eye from anywhere

From the booth PC, the office or your phone, on the cinema network or over VPN. Everyone with their own user.

FAQ

What people ask us most

What happens if the GiuLi PC goes off?

Shows already scheduled start anyway: they are in the schedule of the screen servers. When the PC is back on, GiuLi resumes reading everything by itself.

Can GiuLi stop a show or switch off a projector by mistake?

No. GiuLi does not run commands on its own: manual commands ask for confirmation and respect the server's schedule lock. A projector with the light on is never sent to Eco.

Does it need an internet connection?

No, GiuLi works on the cinema network. Internet is only needed for Telegram alerts and KDMs received by email.

What computer does it need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC switched on in the cinema network. Nothing to install: the first start sets everything up by itself.

Can I manage several cinemas?

Yes. Each cinema has its own folder with data and credentials, and the "All cinemas" page shows every screen of every site.

I already use another TMS: what changes?

Playlists and schedules stay on the screen servers. GiuLi reads them, including those with a Doremi Intermission, shows them in the schedule with the KDM checks, and can copy them between screens or import them into its own playlists.

What languages does it speak?

English and Italian: switch with the flag at the top, on the login page too.
